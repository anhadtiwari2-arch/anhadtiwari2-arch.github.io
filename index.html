THE COST ADVANTAGE

Low operating cost.
Practical business value.

Developed in house to keep costs low, with attendance, reporting and quiz activities in one place. This reduces the need for separate tools and repeated administration.

The value is time returned to coaching, clearer evidence for leaders and easier access to learning for team members.

QUIZ UPDATE

Easier to join. More engaging to play. More useful to follow up.

A simpler mobile home screen, clearer start controls and improved code entry support a smoother start. Live countdowns, music, visual effects, emoji avatars and leaderboards bring energy to group sessions, while self-paced play gives team members flexibility. Departmental questions and answers shared in advance help build knowledge and confidence; results help trainers plan the next refresher.
